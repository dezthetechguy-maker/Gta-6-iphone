VIDEO MENUS

Put local videos in this folder. The app automatically plays a matching video when you select a menu item.

Supported menu names:
- story.mp4 or story_mode.mp4
- free_roam.mp4 (also freeroam.mp4 / free-roam.mp4)
- characters.mp4 or character_select.mp4
- map.mp4 or world_map.mp4
- options.mp4 or settings.mp4
- developer.mp4 or dev.mp4

Supported extensions: MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI, WEBM (actual playback depends on the media codecs available to Qt/Windows).

When a matching video does not exist, the normal screen opens instead. Videos are local only.
